## IndieWeb

- [IndieWeb](https://indieweb.org/) — Community for owning your content on the web.
- [Webmention.io](https://webmention.io/) — Webmention receiver as a service.
- [POSSE](https://indieweb.org/POSSE) — Publish on your Own Site, Syndicate Elsewhere. The publishing model this site runs on.

## Civic Technology

- [Code for Canada](https://codefor.ca/) — Civic tech fellowship and community.
- [Civic Tech Toronto](https://civictech.ca/) — Weekly hacknights and a running conversation about technology in the public interest, close to home.

## Knowledge Tools

- [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/) — Local-first knowledge management.
- [Eleventy](https://www.11ty.dev/) — The static site generator underneath this site; small, durable, no lock-in.

## Thinking

- [Tools for Conviviality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tools_for_Conviviality) — Ivan Illich's argument for tools that serve people rather than systems. Much of the garden's design ethic traces back here.
