First post on sebthecanadian.ca as I start adding IndieWeb conventions.

This is the beginning of building out a personal site with durable identity, portable publishing, and clear ownership of work. The site uses semantic HTML with microformats (`h-card`, `h-entry`, `h-feed`), webmention discovery, and a POSSE publishing model where writing originates in the [garden](https://cognitivearchitecture.ca/) and is syndicated here as excerpts.

More to come as the site takes shape.
